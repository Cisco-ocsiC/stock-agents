To hit $300/week passively you need **~$450K invested**.

## Three Realistic Paths from Here

### Path A: Grow Capital First (recommended)
- Invest $2,500 → target 8-12%/year return
- Add $X/month from your income
- Reinvest ALL dividends back in
- At $300/mo added, reach $15K in 4 years → $300/wk passive within 8-10 yrs
- **This is how 99% of investors actually build income.**

### Path B: Learn Swing Trading
- Use $2,500 as training capital, not passive income source
- Target 3-8% monthly (12-96% yearly), NOT 12%/week
- Realistic drawdowns: -20% to -40% on bad months
- Full-time job required to manage properly
- Only move profits to "income account" after proven track record

### Path C: High-Yield Risky Plays (NOT recommended for essential income)
- Penny stocks, leveraged ETFs, crypto staking
- Potential for high weekly gains BUT equally likely to lose 50-80% quickly
- This is speculation/gambling, not investing
